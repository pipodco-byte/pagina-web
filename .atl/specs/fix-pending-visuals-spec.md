# Spec: fix-pending-visuals

## Typography/Global
### Requirement: Font smoothing
`html *` MUST have `-webkit-font-smoothing: antialiased` and `-moz-osx-font-smoothing: grayscale`

## UI/Buttons
### Requirement: Button pill shape
`.btn.btn-dark` SHOULD have `border-radius: 50px` and subtle shadow

## Layout/Navbar
### Requirement: Nav item weight
`.nav-item` SHOULD be `font-weight: 600` (previously 500)

## Typography/Product
### Requirement: Product title weight
`h2` in product overview SHOULD be `font-weight: 700`
